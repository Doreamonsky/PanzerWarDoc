    Vehicle modding notes

    中文 | English

    Original notes by @情绪薫.

    Models and pivots

    Check the specific asset's modification and distribution license before buying or downloading it. Place the turret pivot on the traverse axis, the Gun pivot on the elevation axis, and Dym at the barrel's intended origin. Wheel pivots belong on the axles. A turretless vehicle can keep an empty turret node for the gun hierarchy. See ground vehicle creation and axis correction.

    Visibility and collision

    • If internal components are missing, check the VehicleInfo module configuration and hitbox naming. If the model is missing, check its visibility, layer, material, and camera mask. See model visibility troubleshooting.
    • For transparent areas in the armor viewer, check normals, colliders, and armor groups. See reactive armor for those parts. If impact effects are too large, check shell caliber and effect parameters.
    • If a mod is missing, check vehicle troubleshooting and the runtime log, then follow the error to the package platform or the asset reference.

    Movement and detail

    • If the vehicle flips in the garage or pitches excessively under acceleration or braking, investigate mass, center of gravity, wheel contact, and friction. Test wheel physics in an appropriate scene.
    • For radar rotation and similar animations, put the animated mesh under a separate pivot. Track nodes follow the road wheels and define the track path. Check gun elevation limits across headings in game.
    • See legacy multiple turrets for existing roof and coaxial guns. See vehicle thumbnails for preview images.
