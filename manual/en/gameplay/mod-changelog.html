    Mod Changelog (4–8 May 2022)

    中文 | English

    This archive covers May 2022. At the time, the guide required version 0.9.6 to update those mods. For installation and update steps, see the mod installation guide.

    2022.5.8

    1. Delivered the modular version of the SU 100 Y for testing (Steam Workshop).
    2. The SU 100 Y is changed to a tier 5 vehicle. Reload time cut down to 17.5 seconds and single-shot damage increased, so that it can one-shot vehicles of the same tier.
    3. Delivered the health-bar version of the T67 for testing (Android).

    2022.5.7

    1. Delivered the modular version of the SU 100 Y for testing (Android).

    2022.5.6

    1. SU 100 Y: testing PBR materials.

    2022.5.5

    1. Delivered the health-bar version of the SU 100 Y for testing (Android).

    2022.5.4

    1. Fixed the wrong armor type on the Maus's tracks and fenders.
    2. Fixed one of the ST-II's guns having no sound effect.
    3. T-54: mobility improved and suspension behavior refined.
