    Class RadarAPI

    雷达 API：读取载具雷达（地面防空雷达或机载雷达）的状态、目标与告警，并控制本地玩家自己的雷达。雷达参数在载具数据的 groundAARadarData（飞机为 radarData）里配置。联机远程副本的雷达不在本机扫描：没有目标，扫描方位不动，锁定目标与工作模式来自网络，锁定 / 模式事件不触发。 Radar API: read a vehicle's radar (ground air-defence or airborne): status, contacts and warnings, and control the local player's own radar. Radar parameters are set in the vehicle data's groundAARadarData (radarData for aircraft). A remote copy's radar does not scan on this client: no contacts, a still scan azimuth, lock target and work mode from the network, and no lock / mode events.

    GetContact(BaseRadarSystem, int, out BaseInitSystem, out float, out float, out float, out float, out float, out bool)

    读取第 index 个目标（从 0 开始）。数据是波束上次扫过它时的，两次扫过之间不变。方位角、俯仰角是雷达坐标系下的（度，0 为正前方，方位向右为正，俯仰向上为正）：地面防空雷达以炮塔为准；水平稳定的机载雷达方位从机头航向量起、俯仰从地平线量起。 Read contact number index (0-based). Values are from the last time the beam swept it and stay put between sweeps. Azimuth and elevation are in the radar's frame, in degrees (0 straight ahead, azimuth positive to the right, elevation positive up): the turret for ground air defence; a horizon-stabilized airborne radar measures azimuth from the nose heading and elevation from the horizon.

    GetScanAzimuth(BaseRadarSystem)

    获取天线当前方位角（度，-180~180，0 为正前方，向右为正）。正前方指雷达安装处的朝向：地面防空雷达是炮塔，机载雷达是机头（水平稳定的雷达取机头在水平面上的朝向）。扇扫雷达在 ±扇区半角之间往返，全向旋转雷达一圈圈转，跟踪（TRK）时指向锁定目标。把它作为天线模型相对安装处的偏航角即可让模型跟着转。 Get the antenna's current azimuth in degrees, -180 to 180, 0 straight ahead, positive to the right. Ahead is where the radar is mounted: the turret for ground air defence, the nose for aircraft (a horizon-stabilized radar uses the nose heading in the horizontal plane). A sector radar sweeps back and forth within its half-angle, a full-rotation radar spins, and in TRK it points at the locked target. Use it as your antenna model's yaw relative to its mount to turn the model with it.
