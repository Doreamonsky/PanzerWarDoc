    RtsNetGameMode

    Online RTS (BattleUnitOwnership.md §25, server docs/rts.md): every player picks a lineup within the budget per wave and deploys it whole; the server lays it out at one of their side's spawn points. Once all of a player's units are gone they wait out the cooldown and deploy again. Units are ordered from the map (the offline commander's RtsCommandController) or driven one at a time. The server owns the clock, the prices, the scores and the outcome. A unit's last order rides its sync state, so whoever simulates it next (the host for a player who left, the owner after a rejoin) carries it on.

    WarZoneNetGameMode

    War zone network game mode: the capture zone map, except a sortie is bought rather than granted. Every price is the server's; this only shows the table it resolved. A room may let a player keep several vehicles (BattleUnitOwnership.md §16.3 A7): while alive they buy more at the usual price, each holding at its spawn point until driven.
